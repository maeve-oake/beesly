The app draws the Luna-inspired window frame, button faces and appliance artwork
at runtime. Only the original shell icons and typefaces are bundled as assets.
Caption commands follow Microsoft's guidelines; Close is implemented, while
unsupported minimize/maximize/help buttons are omitted:
